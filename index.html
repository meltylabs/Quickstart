July planner

Expected temps, weather, and altitude

July is one of Uganda's drier travel months. The biggest comfort shift is altitude: Murchison and Queen Elizabeth feel warmer, while Bwindi and Bunyonyi are cooler in the morning and evening.

Day Location Altitude Expected July temp Basic July weather
Day 1 EntebbeLake Victoria arrival 1,180 m / 3,870 ft 18-25°C / 64-77°F Warm, breezy, humid, with occasional light showers.
Day 2 Kampala to ZiwaNorthbound transfer and rhino tracking Kampala 1,200 m; Nakasongola/Ziwa approx. 1,090 m 16-28°C / 61-82°F Generally dry-season travel weather; warm afternoons and mild mornings.
Days 3-4 Murchison Falls National ParkNile falls, delta, and savanna 619-1,292 m / 2,031-4,239 ft 19-30°C / 66-86°F Lowland safari conditions: sunny, dry spells, hot afternoons, cooler nights.
Day 5 Kibale Forest / Fort PortalChimpanzee tracking zone Kibale 1,100-1,590 m; Fort Portal 1,523 m 16-26°C / 61-79°F Mild forest climate; showers are possible even during the drier window.
Days 6-7 Queen Elizabeth National ParkKazinga Channel and Ishasha About 880-1,350 m in the main park; average near 1,194 m 17-28°C / 63-82°F One of the driest periods; warm days, cool mornings, slight rain chance.
Days 8-9 Bwindi Impenetrable National ParkGorilla trekking 1,160-2,607 m / 3,806-8,553 ft 12-21°C / 54-70°F Cool, damp montane forest; trails are usually drier than in rainy season.
Day 10 Lake BunyonyiHighland lake and return prep 1,962 m / 6,437 ft 10-22°C / 50-72°F Cool mornings, pleasant afternoons, lake breezes, and crisp evenings.

Temperatures are planning averages, not guarantees. Pack light layers, a rain shell, sun protection, and warmer clothing for Bwindi and Lake Bunyonyi mornings.
