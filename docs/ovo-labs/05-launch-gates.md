# OVO Labs — Launch Gates

This checklist distinguishes a complete fictional storefront from a company authorized to transact. “Not started” is the truthful default for every operating gate.

## Gate 0 — Fictional storefront release

- [ ] One fiction banner appears on every route.
- [ ] No checkout, payment, order, quote, reservation, or live lead capture exists.
- [ ] Every concept price is understood as a UI assumption, not an offer.
- [ ] Demo cart add, quantity, remove, clear, persistence, and end state work.
- [ ] Search finds every name, code, category, and documented synonym.
- [ ] Collection filters, applied state, reset, sort, and zero-results behavior work.
- [ ] Every PDP uses the same complete decision block.
- [ ] Every testing surface says “No result reported.”
- [ ] No fabricated review, lab, COA, lot, test, purity, customer, stock, shipping, or support claim.
- [ ] No dosing, administration, preparation, body-outcome, safety, efficacy, or human-use content.
- [ ] No Product, Offer, AggregateRating, stock, or shipping schema.
- [ ] Accessibility, mobile, performance, security, link, and console QA pass.
- [ ] Fable 5 reviews rendered desktop and narrow-width evidence and gives an explicit ship verdict.

## Gate A — Legal and intended use

- [ ] Exact entity and beneficial ownership established and disclosed.
- [ ] Specialized FDA/FDCA counsel reviews each exact product and format.
- [ ] Counsel reviews the full website, search terms, content, ads, emails, account flow, and customer journey—not just the disclaimer.
- [ ] Written jurisdiction and sales-channel scope exists.
- [ ] Importer-of-record and customs plan is approved where applicable.
- [ ] Terms, privacy, access, returns, complaints, withdrawal, and recall policies are reviewed.
- [ ] No product, copy, imagery, cross-sell, testimonial, or channel implies prohibited human use.
- [ ] Counsel confirms which product concepts must be removed.

**Fatal gate:** no commerce if any material, claim, or audience is unresolved.

## Gate B — Quality and evidence

- [ ] Actual manufacturer and site verified.
- [ ] Supplier diligence and sanctions screening complete.
- [ ] Quality agreement and change control signed.
- [ ] Specifications versioned and approved.
- [ ] Independent testing pathways contracted.
- [ ] Sample identity and chain of custody designed and tested.
- [ ] Original reports and data-access rights established.
- [ ] Review, rejection, supersession, and withdrawal states implemented.
- [ ] Item-to-record matching passes.
- [ ] Complaint, deviation, withdrawal, and recall exercises pass.

**Fatal gate:** no quality or test claim without a traceable exact-item record.

## Gate C — Financial counterparties

- [ ] Bank receives the exact entity, owners, catalog, supplier geography, audience, expected volume, and operating flow.
- [ ] Processor receives the same exact information.
- [ ] Insurers receive the exact catalog, claims, storage, and fulfillment model.
- [ ] Tax, accounting, chargeback, refund, and reserve policies are approved.
- [ ] No application uses a vague or misleading category such as generic ecommerce or supplements.
- [ ] Written acceptance is retained.

**Fatal gate:** no commerce if approval depends on omission, euphemism, or misclassification.

## Gate D — Operations

- [ ] Storage and fulfillment partners approve the exact materials.
- [ ] Receiving, custody, storage, pick, pack, and shipment controls are tested.
- [ ] Inventory and cycle-count reconciliation pass.
- [ ] Order-to-cash reconciliation passes.
- [ ] Support, escalation, complaint, and exception coverage is staffed.
- [ ] Recipient/item/record mapping passes.
- [ ] Security, access, backup, and incident-response reviews pass.

**Fatal gate:** no shipment without end-to-end traceability and containment.

## Gate E — Website and data truth

- [ ] Every operating claim maps to current source records and an owner.
- [ ] Every real report maps to the exact represented item.
- [ ] Concept examples are removed or unmistakably isolated.
- [ ] Prices, availability, taxes, terms, and policies are accurate.
- [ ] No fake scarcity, reviews, ratings, popularity, or customer counts.
- [ ] Data collection is minimal, consented, secured, and documented.
- [ ] Advertising systems receive no sensitive research or health information.
- [ ] Accessibility, mobile, performance, and security pass before release.
- [ ] Fable 5 reviews the activated commerce states at desktop and narrow widths.

## Gate F — Controlled pilot

- [ ] Exact pilot audience and cap approved.
- [ ] Manual dual review live.
- [ ] Every item has a complete traceability record.
- [ ] Every order, fee, settlement, record, and shipment reconciles.
- [ ] Complaint, withdrawal, and recall contacts are live.
- [ ] No unsupported claim incident.
- [ ] Pilot stop conditions and authority are documented.

## Release authority

| Release | Required sign-off |
|---|---|
| Fictional storefront | Product, QA, claims, accessibility, Fable 5 |
| Content with scientific/regulatory facts | Claims editor and appropriate subject-matter/legal review |
| Analytics change | Product, privacy/security |
| Any commercial pilot | Counsel, quality, finance, insurer, bank, processor, operations, security, executive owner |
