# OVO Labs — Strategy Package

OVO Labs is a fictional peptide retail-house concept with the wordmark `ovolabs.` and descriptor “Peptide science, refined.” It has no inventory, tests, lots, customers, orders, checkout, payment, fulfillment, or offer to sell. The public prototype uses concept prices and a browser-local demo cart to test a complete ecommerce journey without creating commerce.

## Evidence grades

- **A:** official regulator or directly observable first-party interface.
- **B:** independent research or a large-scale benchmark with disclosed method.
- **C:** vendor-, platform-, or agency-published case study; directional.
- **D:** anonymous or unverified secondary signal; vocabulary and idea generation only.
- **H:** OVO Labs hypothesis or model assumption; must be tested.

## Non-negotiable truth rules

- No fabricated reviews, laboratories, purity, stock, shipping, customers, results, reports, or urgency.
- “No result reported” is the default testing state.
- No dosing, preparation, administration, body-outcome, safety, efficacy, or human-use content.
- Safe concept imagery only: no syringes, preparation supplies, body context, fake reports, invented labels, or use-adjacent props.
- Demo cart only; no checkout.
- Every visible control works.
- Every factual claim is observable, cited, labeled as an assumption, or removed.
- Fable 5 must review rendered desktop and narrow-width evidence before outward-facing shipment.
