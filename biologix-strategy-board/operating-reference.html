03 / Role progression

This is an Operating Partner mandate, not a CRO role

Company-wide responsibility requires company-wide authority, economics, protection, and a clear operating perimeter.

Strategic inference: Working structure

Source-of-Truth Sprint through OVO Labs

Contractual and non-statutory during proof. Alex owns the sprint, architecture, instrumentation, founder-relief plan, operating cadence, and permanent-deal recommendation. Product release, customer funds, manufacturing, import, clinical decisions, and legal conclusions stay outside his scope.

Gate · data clears + authority and economics signed + indemnification, advancement, and insurance reviewed
Required proof: Not earned yet

Activated Operating Partner through OVO Labs

Appropriate only if Alex is truly accountable for company architecture, finance, people, systems, creator distribution, lifecycle, content, management, and executive cadence—with the decision rights and upside required to control those outcomes. Braden retains product, sourcing, inventory, and fulfillment responsibility.

05 / Durable boundary

The cash engine and the durable company are different assets

Legacy cash can fund the future. It should not automatically own every audience, software, care, data, and media asset Alex creates.

Assets proposed for Durable NewCo versus Legacy CashCo.
Lives in Durable NewCo — substantially Alex-ownedStays in Legacy CashCo unless separately agreed
  • Owned audience, media, founder content system, newsletter, and community.
  • Creator and affiliate operating software, contribution ledger, lifecycle infrastructure, and generalized workflows.
  • Customer-development data and permissions created under the NewCo scope.
  • Merchant-neutral research, creator graph, brand relationships, and operator network.
  • Appropriately licensed diagnostics, care orchestration, software, education, and lawful future products.
  • NewCo brand, IP, contracts, books, bank relationships, and reserved-matter governance.
  • Historical storefront, catalog, customer liabilities, product claims, and legacy transactions.
  • Existing inventory, supplier relationships, import history, testing records, complaints, and fulfillment obligations.
  • Historical processor, bank, tax, owner, employee, vendor, and regulatory obligations.
  • Legacy domain, storefront code, trademarks, contracts, and data not expressly licensed or transferred.
  • Legacy owner distributions and pre-closing economics.
  • Any risk Alex did not diligence, price, accept, and document.
06 / Architecture reveal policy

Show the silhouette of the machine, not its wiring

Free structure proves command. The finished schemas, creator graph, SOPs, controls, vendor design, and implementation mechanics are the paid asset.

Alex should reveal enough to make the before-and-after obvious: the diagnosis, system categories, sample scorecard, founder-relief sequence, ninety-day destination, evidence gates, and economic logic. Every additional layer should be exchanged for reciprocal access and commitment.

  1. Before payment

    Diagnosis, current-state gaps, workstreams, sample scorecard, expected outputs, and the ninety-day milestones.

  2. After NDA and source access

    Company map, data request, KPI definitions, decision logic, high-level workflows, and the sprint delivery plan.

  3. After payment or definitive agreement

    Schemas, automations, creator lists, attribution logic, vendor stack, recruiting pipeline, legal templates, playbooks, software, and reusable implementation IP.
