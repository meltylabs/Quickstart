04 / Operating system

The first twenty-five creators are a designed cohort

Do not scale a public affiliate free-for-all. Prove contribution and claims discipline with one cohort before adding volume.

Five anchors. Ten converters. Ten niche testers.

The proposition is a premium content and offer team, transparent attribution, approved twice-monthly payouts, founder access, fast creative support, and better economics earned through contribution quality.

Creator control

Commission base

  • Gross product revenue less discounts, refunds, chargebacks, taxes, and excluded shipping.
  • Approve only after processor settlement and the return or dispute hold.
  • Every creator receives an order, deduction, reversal, rate, and payout statement.
Source truth

One financial language

  • Orders → processor → bank → inventory → shipping.
  • Contribution by SKU, creator, cohort, and lot.
  • Revenue, gross profit, operating contribution, and cash never blur.
Lifecycle + quality

Every order creates data

  • Delivery, satisfaction, repeat, review, referral, and support cohorts.
  • Inventory by ordered, quarantined, released, allocated, returned, and recalled status.
  • Four-hour lot-to-customer and customer-to-lot traceability target.
ReferenceBackend phases — build truth before softwareExpand
  1. Truth sprint: read-only exports, order-to-settlement bridge, product, creator, refund, and inventory views.
  2. First thirty days: contribution ledger, creator payout ledger, lot registry, claims queue, and weekly management pack.
  3. Days thirty-one to ninety: stable connectors, statements, cohort reporting, live-asset archive, and issue workflows.
  4. After repeatability: multi-tenant control plane and external merchant onboarding. No SaaS theater before a second design partner.

Operating rule · WooCommerce, the processor, and the 3PL remain systems of transaction until the replacement case is proven.

Why 85% is probably product gross margin

The rest of the company still costs money.

  • At 85% true net, every monthly expense would fit inside $240,934 at the extrapolated pace.
  • If four creators each earned $10K–$12K weekly, payouts alone would be about $173K–$208K.
  • That leaves almost nothing for product, processing, shipping, labor, testing, support, legal, and reserves.
Liquidity reality

Contribution is not available cash.

  • Illustrative simultaneous-stress buffer · approximately $0.9M–$1.2M before taxes.
  • Processor holds, inventory lead time, affiliate payables, refund reserves, and staffing all consume cash.
  • The extrapolation assumes fractional and outsourced functions; real staffing reprices it dollar for dollar.
