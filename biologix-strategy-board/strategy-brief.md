# Biologix opportunity: decision brief

## North star

Turn an accidental cash engine into owned, durable leverage without letting it consume OVO or inheriting unverified liabilities.

This is not automatically shiny-object syndrome. Alex has privileged founder access, Connor's trust, creator and affiliate relationships, OVO Talent distribution, OVO Academy operating capacity, content infrastructure, systems skill, and the ability to assemble the missing company layer quickly.

It becomes shiny-object syndrome if Alex reacts to Braden's reported economics by cloning the easiest surface layer before proving settled cash, repeat demand, distribution, product and quality records, ownership, or a usable lawful path.

## The leverage lesson

OVO Talent is a labor-intensive service layer. Someone else owns the customer transaction and product, while OVO captures only a fraction of the value it creates. Each new campaign adds coordination and headcount.

Inner DM can create software leverage, but software without massive distribution takes time to compound.

Biologix appears to occupy a more leveraged economic position: the customer pays the merchant directly, the owner captures the markup, a website can transact around the clock, and repeat purchasing may exist. This is not proof that Braden is smarter. It is proof that ownership of the customer, transaction, data, and equity matters more than the volume of labor performed.

## Recommended sequence

1. Enter through a paid operating sprint.
2. Get complete order, customer, product, referral, settlement, inventory, partner, ownership, and vendor data.
3. Reverse-engineer the word-of-mouth loop.
4. Remove Braden from fulfillment.
5. Build lead capture, referral, lifecycle, support, and analytics.
6. Negotiate meaningful economics for converting accidental demand into a company.
7. Put durable audience, media, software, data, community, and appropriately licensed care infrastructure into a clean NewCo Alex substantially owns.
8. Preserve a narrow contractual right to pursue other lawful longevity opportunities if the partnership ends.

## Deal sequence

- **Stage 1: paid source-of-truth sprint.** Target approximately $50,000 prepaid. No officer title, OVO creators, OVO IP transfer, inventory capital, personal guarantees, or custody of customer or product funds.
- **Stage 2: Legacy CashCo operating economics.** Only after verification: meaningful monthly cash, contribution-profit participation, protected equity or option economics, information and audit rights, distribution protection, and authority that matches accountability.
- **Stage 3: Durable NewCo.** Separate cap table, books, data, contracts, merchant relationships, IP, audience, software, content, community, and appropriately licensed diagnostics or care rails. Alex receives cofounder-level ownership if OVO builds the operating platform.
