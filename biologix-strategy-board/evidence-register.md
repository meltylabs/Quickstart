**Research date:** July 22, 2026

**Purpose:** Make the changing public snapshots and founder-reported inputs reproducible without treating them as verified financial records.

## Public popularity and price snapshot

- Captured: **2026-07-22 18:19:22 PDT**.
- Endpoint: [Biologix WooCommerce Store API, popularity descending](https://biologixlabsresearch.com/wp-json/wc/store/v1/products?per_page=100&orderby=popularity&order=desc).
- Stored derivative: [`public-demand-map.csv`](./public-demand-map.csv).
- Publicly observed fields: rank/order returned by the endpoint, product label, current minimum price, and current maximum variant price.
- Not observable from the endpoint: units sold, revenue, customers, time period represented by popularity, repeat rate, contribution, or whether historical sales metadata moved during the platform migration.
- Analyst-created fields: demand cluster and modeled portfolio role.

Prices, rank, stock, and product availability are dynamic. A later request may differ without implying that either snapshot was false.

## Evidence labels

- **Publicly observed:** directly visible in a timestamped public source.
- **Private founder signal:** directly observed in a user-authorized private communication, but not proof of financial or contractual claims.
- **Founder-reported — unverified:** said or shown by Braden without source-record reconciliation.
- **Strategic inference:** analyst interpretation from observed/reported facts.
- **Modeled assumption:** an input selected for sensitivity analysis, not a statement about Biologix.
- **Required proof:** a record or legal/quality conclusion needed before money, title, equity, or operating responsibility changes hands.
