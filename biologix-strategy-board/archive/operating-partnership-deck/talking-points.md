# Braden meeting guide

## The objective

The win is not a verbal promise of equity. The win is:

1. Braden sees Alex as the person who can build the company around what he created.
2. Braden understands why Operating Partner, Growth & Scale is broader than a CRO, affiliate manager, or agency role.
3. Both parties choose a staged structure instead of arguing from unverified screenshots.
4. Braden approves a $50,000 prepaid 30-day source-of-truth sprint and data-access session.

Do not accept a public title, launch OVO creators, take custody of money or product, or commit to the permanent package on the call.

## Ask before opening the deck

- “If the business doubled next month and nothing operational changed, what breaks first?”
- “What parts of the company do you personally never want to touch again?”
- “If this worked perfectly, what would your actual week and life look like twelve months from now?”

Use his answers throughout the pitch.

## Why equity and performance participation can coexist

They pay for different value:

- earned equity pays Alex for becoming a long-term owner and building durable enterprise value;
- the $30,000 monthly amount pays for Alex's personal Operating Partner mandate;
- the separate $20,000 OVO deployment fee pays for a managed pod, delivery responsibility, and replacement capacity;
- the time-limited incremental contribution share pays cash during a risky operating window only when verified economics improve.

Twenty-five percent equity plus twenty-five percent of all revenue is not the smart version. At $1.5 million in monthly revenue, the revenue share alone would be $375,000 per month. Depending on true margin, adding 25% ownership can give Alex roughly half or more of total profit. Braden would reasonably see that as taking credit for the engine he already built.

## Economic definitions

### Net Collected Revenue

Cash actually settled from customer transactions, minus only:

- sales tax;
- refunds;
- chargebacks;
- customer credits;
- agreed shipping pass-through.

It is not reduced by inventory purchases, hiring, owner spending, marketing, or discretionary reinvestment.

### Collected Contribution Profit

Net Collected Revenue minus a locked schedule of direct order-level costs:

- landed COGS for units actually sold;
- affiliate commissions;
- processor fees;
- packaging;
- pick/pack and outbound shipping;
- direct reships and agreed variable support cost.

It does not subtract unsold inventory purchases, capex, owner compensation, related-party fees, or discretionary hires unless both parties approve the category.

### Baseline

Lock before Alex starts. Use settled source data, not a peak screenshot. Proposed definition:

> The lower of median weekly Collected Contribution Profit over the prior eight fully settled weeks multiplied by 4.345, or average monthly Collected Contribution Profit over the prior 90 days.

### Available Cash

Settled cash after current ordinary payables, taxes, refund and chargeback reserves, and a specifically capped working-capital/inventory reserve.

“We want to reinvest it” is not itself a reserve.

### Fully diluted ownership

Alex's percentage after counting every issued or promised unit, option, warrant, convertible instrument, and reserved employee pool.

## Objection handling

### “Someone already asked for 25% for posts.”

> That deal taxed every existing sale immediately for temporary posts. Lane 2 applies only to accepted creators I introduce. The hybrid applies performance participation only above the locked baseline. True equity participates in the entire company, which is why the ownership-heavy package carries no performance share and only accompanies the full company-building mandate.

### “Why should I give up equity when it already works?”

> You should not give up equity to preserve the status quo. Equity only makes sense if you want a true operating partner who creates durable value, removes dependence on you, and assumes responsibility for building the next company. If you want to preserve equity, choose the cash-performance structure.

### “Why do you need to see everything?”

> Because I cannot responsibly price my role, allocate a team, or calculate performance from storefront screenshots. Trust lets us move quickly. Reconciliation protects the relationship.

### “Start recruiting creators now.”

> I can move quickly with the current channel, but recruiting before attribution, approval rules, payout logic, and contribution truth would scale disorder. Activation installs the minimum control layer first.

### “I am reinvesting everything.”

> That is why the agreement separates inventory, reserves, contribution, and discretionary reinvestment. My earned cash is paid before optional reinvestment. Otherwise a profitable company can make ownership economically meaningless.

### “Just be a consultant on paper.”

> The contract needs to match what I actually do. During activation I am a genuinely limited Operating Partner (Designate). If I later take the permanent mandate, the documents need real authority, insurance, advancement, stop rights, and an approved operating perimeter. A label is not protection.

### “The business is research-use only.”

> I am not making that legal conclusion. Independent FDA/FTC counsel needs to define what can operate, what must change, and what must stop before my title or growth mandate begins.

## Negotiation discipline

- Present the two lanes cleanly and make Braden choose the relationship before negotiating percentages.
- The lanes do not blend. No backend build, operating work, team, or management at introduction-lane pricing.
- Never reduce cash, equity, and performance participation simultaneously.
- Every equity concession buys additional guaranteed cash, performance participation, or purchase rights.
- Friendship and community improve the partnership; they are not compensation.
- Connor is strengthened inside the operating system, not casually displaced.
- OVO Academy, OVO Talent, generalized systems, and creators are not contributed to Biologix.
- No broad health, longevity, creator, media, software, or telehealth noncompete.
- A title without access, authority, distributions, insurance, payment seniority, and exit rights is a no.

## The role

During activation:

- Operating Partner (Designate);
- no public officer appointment;
- read-only access and nonbinding recommendations;
- no creators, product claims, money or product custody, inventory, prescribing, or medical guidance;
- no authority over sourcing, suppliers, vendors, fulfillment, shipping, or the 3PL.

After financial, legal, insurance, and operating conditions clear:

- Operating Partner, Growth & Scale;
- Braden remains Founder/CEO and retains product, category, sourcing, suppliers, vendors, inventory, fulfillment, shipping, and 3PL authority within the agreed perimeter;
- Alex leads the company-building operating plan, financial instrumentation, organization, hiring, creator and affiliate backend, lifecycle systems, analytics, founder media, creator ecosystem, management cadence, and controlled scale;
- reserved matters remain shared.

The title is not legal insulation. Actual conduct and authority matter more than the label. Alex should not accept the permanent role unless he has real stop authority, independent counsel, advancement, insurance, complete information, and the ability to resign if a compliance stop is overridden.
