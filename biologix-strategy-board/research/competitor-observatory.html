03 / Evidence rules

Know what you know.

The page is useful only if estimates stay estimates and missing evidence stays unknown.

Verified

Primary registry or direct record.

Observed

Captured on a public page, feed, code path, or screenshot.

Estimated

A disclosed model, never measured revenue or analytics.

Unknown

No defensible public answer. Not zero.
