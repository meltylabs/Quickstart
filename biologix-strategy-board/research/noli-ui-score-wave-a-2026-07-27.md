## NOLI takeaways

1. Use one compact mobile decision card: identity, strength, quantity, price, per-mg or per-vial economics, stock, CTA, and delivery.
2. Show current-lot proof, not a generic COA badge: lot, test date, lab, identity, purity, mass, endotoxin, residual solvents, and link to the report.
3. Keep proof visible without hiding the purchase state. BulkGLP has excellent proof but loses mobile usability; Compound Sciences has excellent usability but weak proof.
4. Treat mobile overlays, menus, cookie notices, sticky bars, and gate screens as first-class product surfaces.
5. Simple scales: reuse one product template, one proof card, one bundle selector, and one policy strip. Do not build a dense page for every possible claim.
