# Peptide landscape source audit

**Audit date:** July 26, 2026  
**Scope:** all seven research artifacts in `.context/peptide-company-landscape/`, 267 unique linked URLs, and the private claims raised in the working conversation.  
**Purpose:** stop repeated anecdotes, sales claims, screenshots, and broker listings from being mistaken for independent proof.

## Bottom line

The demand signal is real. The public evidence does not yet prove durable net margins, a completed eight-figure peptide exit, or a compliant consumer RUO growth model using Noli's access to OVO Talent's lifestyle creator network.

The most important correction is categorical:

> The cited BlackHatWorld thread does not document a $15 million offer for a peptide company. It documents an anonymous broker saying he had $15 million of unrelated hard-money real-estate deals to place while separately discussing peptide payment processing.

The July 23 and 24 FDA advisory vote is also easy to overstate. The committee recommended adding six peptide substance families to the 503A Bulks List. The recommendation was non-binding. It did not approve the substances, put them on the list immediately, or make retatrutide compoundable.

## Decision rules

- **KEEP:** supported at the level stated.
- **QUALIFY:** useful, but the wording must identify the source type, uncertainty, snapshot date, or inference.
- **REMOVE:** contradicted, unsupported, misleading, or too stale to use as a current claim.

## Customer-voice and forum evidence QA

1. The stated "44 distinct 2025 to 2026 pages" includes at least one source labeled a 2024 to 2025 baseline. Change the scope to "44-page core weighted to 2025 and 2026, with one 2024 to 2025 payment-recourse baseline," or remove that page from the core count.
2. Frequency counts are directional manual page-level coding, not statistically representative market incidence. State that the coding was not independently replicated.
3. Trustpilot ratings and review totals are volatile snapshots. Keep the capture date next to each number.
4. Anonymous first-person forum reports can establish that a claim was made and reveal vocabulary or failure modes. They cannot establish market prevalence, legality, normalized margins, or processor policy.
5. "First-order conversion rises when..." overstates qualitative evidence. Use "Buyers repeatedly praise..." unless an A/B test or cohort analysis supports causality.
6. "A successful replacement often produces stronger loyalty than flawless fulfillment" also overstates causality. Use: "Several reviewers describe replacements alongside repeat loyalty, suggesting that a clear remedy can protect trust."
7. Named allegations about specific vendors must remain attributed, dated, and bounded. Never turn a complaint, forum post, or third-party analyst's concern into a finding of fraud or product failure.

## Cross-artifact contradictions to resolve

1. **RUO proof wedge versus Noli creators:** the master dossier recommends a research-product store as a fast wedge, while the regulatory and customer-voice work correctly says creator content can establish human intended use. The latter controls. No consumer RUO creator pilot.
2. **Clinician migration versus retatrutide:** a clinician lane can only include FDA-approved or independently lawful, legally compoundable products. It does not make retatrutide compoundable.
3. **Five versus ten creators:** the operating copy says five; the founder report says ten. Use five for the truth cohort. Add creators only after delivered orders, settled contribution, repeat behavior, support performance, and zero unresolved claims violations.
4. **One versus three offers:** "simple scales" supports one eligible offer for the first cohort, not three simultaneous offers.
5. **Legal lane sequencing:** counsel, molecule status, counterparty approval, payment approval, testing specifications, and fulfillment validation come before SKU and creator recruitment.

## Required evidence before using Braden's numbers

Ask for a read-only, reconciled diligence packet covering the same trailing 90 days:

1. Order-level export with gross sales, discounts, refunds, taxes, and fulfillment status.
2. Processor statements showing authorization volume, rolling reserves, disputes, fees, and net settlements.
3. Bank statements tying settlements to cash.
4. Supplier invoices, payment evidence, freight, customs, failed lots, replacements, and landed released cost by SKU.
5. Third-party test invoices and lot-release records for identity, purity, net content, sterility, and endotoxin where applicable.
6. Inventory roll-forward and shrink.
7. Support payroll, reships, lost shipments, affiliate payouts, paid media, software, legal, insurance, and tax.
8. Customer and channel concentration, repeat cohorts, and shutdown history.
9. For any offer: buyer identity, written indication or LOI, target entity, price, structure, working-capital adjustment, earnout, escrow, exclusivity, proof of funds, and current status.

Until that reconciliation exists, the honest language is:

> The private evidence is strong enough to justify serious diligence. It is not strong enough to state verified revenue, normalized 80% margins, or a $15 million offer.
