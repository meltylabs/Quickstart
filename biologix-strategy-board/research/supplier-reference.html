04 / Analytical testing

“USA tested” can be true and still say nothing about origin

A laboratory report characterizes the submitted sample. Without independent sampling and chain of custody, it does not establish who manufactured it or whether every vial in the lot matches.

United States · Michigan TrustPointe Analytics Purity, identity, content, sterility, endotoxin, and related testing United States · Tennessee Freedom Diagnostics Appears on public peptide analytical reports United States · Arizona MZ Biolabs Public COA testing services United States · Illinois submission Kovera Labs Appears on public peptide analytical reports Czechia · Not USA testing Janoshik Analytical Widely used by gray-market vendors and buyer groups
Claim

99% purity

Usually describes the relative chromatographic purity of detected peptide peaks. It does not mean 99% of everything in the vial is the intended peptide.

Claim

Triple tested

No standard definition. It can mean three methods, three safety dimensions, or three laboratories. The underlying reports must define it.

Claim

USA tested

May prove only that a seller-selected vial reached a U.S. laboratory. It does not prove U.S. manufacture, random sampling, sterility, or lot-wide consistency.

Sampling: TrustPointe explains why one vial cannot establish a large lot’s quality.

Purity: TrustPointe’s purity note separates chromatographic purity from total vial composition.

Sterility: Its USP <71> service describes a 14–21 day process that may require several vials.

Published-COA dataset: A private 2026 Disclosed Labs analysis found high reported purity but materially weaker label-content and sterility coverage.

Packaging similarity is not synthesis proof.

Shared caps, boxes, return addresses, lot formats, and COA templates can identify a common finisher, fulfillment warehouse, reseller, or copied content. They do not uniquely identify the reactor that made the peptide.

06 / Reference conclusions

The answers worth carrying forward

01
Best industrial manufacturing evidence

Sinopep-Allsino, followed by Hybio.

02
Closest credible integrated retatrutide platform

Fujian Genohope, with unresolved inspection-history and storefront-link questions.

03
Broadest credible gray-overlap factory catalogs

Sichuan Jisheng and Guizhou Utide. Catalog overlap does not prove that either supplies a named seller.

04
Primary gray supplier decision

HK Peptides for the commercial supplier relationship, with Yiwu Aozuo maintained as the qualified backup.

05
No defensible cheapest-factory answer

Public price lists are broker quotes, not auditable factory-gate pricing. MOQs, molecule, testing, fill, packaging, and intermediaries change the quote.

06
The seller-to-factory gap remains real

Resolving it would require authenticated invoices, purchase orders, customs records, original manufacturing batch records, subcontractor disclosure, and audited chain of custody.
