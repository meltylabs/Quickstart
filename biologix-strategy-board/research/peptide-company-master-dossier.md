# Noli peptide company master dossier

Updated: 2026-07-26

Scope: current peptide-company business models, operator forums, payment processing, sourcing, testing, fulfillment, support, brand positioning, creator growth, paid acquisition, retention, and Noli-specific scale architecture.

## Executive verdict

This is a real and potentially attractive business opportunity. It is not attractive because a vial can be sourced cheaply. Cheap product is exactly why the market is crowded.

The opportunity exists because four scarce assets sit above the product:

1. Trusted demand
2. Verifiable lot proof
3. Reliable transaction and fulfillment operations
4. Repeat customer relationships

Noli is the peptide company. It can access an unfair distribution advantage through OVO Talent, Alex's relationships, creator operations, and the OVO Academy operator bench. Noli does not yet have a proven advantage in quality systems, payment underwriting, fulfillment, or customer support. Those are the systems the pilot must earn.

The recommended decision is a conditional go:

- Build one creator-distributed trust engine, not a giant catalog.
- Prove 50 to 75 clean, settled, fulfilled orders before adding software, paid scale, or more SKUs.
- Do not use OVO Talent's consumer creator network to prove a human-use RUO funnel for Noli. Prove either a counsel-cleared qualified-research B2B model or a separate licensed clinical offer.
- Build the brand and owned customer relationship so it can migrate toward a more guided, defensible model as the category professionalizes.

## The market changed in 2026

Three things are happening simultaneously:

### The commodity layer is exploding

Current vendor indexes and community tools track hundreds of sellers, thousands of tests, and a wide price spread. Retail retatrutide prices currently appear from roughly $2 to $15 per milligram on public comparison tools, while wholesale and source-level claims can be below $1 per milligram. Those figures are directional, not a supplier recommendation.

Implication: product access and a storefront are not moats.

### The premium guided layer is attracting capital

- [Noho Labs raised $16 million](https://www.axios.com/pro/health-tech-deals/2026/05/04/coatue-mazzeo-peptides-noho-labs-16-million) for a personalized peptide model.
- [Feel Peptides raised $3 million](https://www.beautyindependent.com/feel-peptides-raises-3m-elevating-branding-accessibility-peptides/) and is building around premium branding, membership, content, community, and influencer distribution.
- Protocole reportedly raised $6 million for a clinician-guided membership model.
- [Superpower](https://www.businessinsider.com/inside-superpower-peptide-startup-people-hotter-smarter-2026-3) is combining annual testing, a personalized marketplace, memberships, and proprietary-product ambitions.

Implication: sophisticated capital is not betting on a generic grid of vials. It is betting on guidance, data, membership, brand, and proprietary access.

### Consumer demand is ahead of understanding

[Fitt Insider](https://insider.fitt.co/peptide-interest-outpaces-infrastructure/) reports low category understanding and strong interest in clinical guidance, especially among younger consumers. Community discussions show the same trust gap through constant questions about vendors, tests, shipping, and support.

Implication: education and trusted navigation are acquisition products.

### The clinician-guided lane may widen, but nothing changed overnight

On July 23 and 24, 2026, an FDA advisory committee recommended six of seven reviewed peptide families for possible inclusion on the Section 503A bulk drug substances list: BPC-157, KPV, TB-500, MOTS-c, Semax, and Epitalon. Emideltide was not recommended. The [FDA meeting record](https://www.fda.gov/advisory-committees/advisory-committee-calendar/july-23-24-2026-meeting-pharmacy-compounding-advisory-committee-07232026) contains the agency briefing materials; [contemporaneous reporting](https://apnews.com/article/7267d0fad0110e7c2ed301d03cf5f0c2) documents the close votes.

This is an important strategic signal, not final FDA action, FDA approval, or blanket permission to prescribe, compound, advertise, or sell the substances. Any change still depends on the agency's final process and the complete federal, state, pharmacy, prescriber, claims, and patient-specific framework.

Implication: preserve the option to migrate qualified demand into a clinician-guided lane. Do not rebuild the plan around an advisory vote before the final rules and counterparties are real.

## The full operator stack

### 1. Product and supplier

The market contains at least four supply models:

| Supply model | Advantage | Hidden risk | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domestic ready stock | Fast pilot, short lead time | May still be imported or brokered | Initial proof |
| White-label / dropship | Minimal capital, fast catalog | No chain control, no differentiation, dependency | Market reconnaissance only |
| Direct overseas source | Lowest quote, customization | Fraud, inconsistent lots, shipping, documentation | Only after deep qualification |
| Qualified manufacturer / fill-finish | Better control and repeatability | Higher MOQ, lead time, cash | Scale after proof |

Public 2026 sites now sell complete white-label systems, including storefronts, labels, COAs, fulfillment, and sometimes payment introductions. Examples include [Pure Chain Logistics](https://purechainlg.com/), [PepFulfill](https://www.pepfulfill.com/), [Pepify](https://pepify.pro/), and [DropShip Peptide](https://dropshippeptide.com/). These pages prove that turnkey supply has become commoditized. They do not prove any provider's quality or suitability.

Supplier qualification should require:

- Verified business identity and facility role
- Exact statement of manufacturer, broker, filler, and shipper
- Current lot number before purchase
- Supplier document packet and raw analytical files where available
- Independent blind sample from the received lot
- Identity plus quantity and purity, not purity alone
- Sterility, endotoxin, bioburden, metals, or solvent work where relevant to the product specification
- Label and carton proof tied to the same lot
- Retention sample
- Change-control notice
- Failed-lot and remediation history
- Replacement, rejection, recall, and indemnity terms
- Capacity, lead-time, and repeat-lot data
- Two genuinely independent sources, not two brokers using the same upstream inventory

The staged sourcing strategy:

1. Pilot with one domestic lot that can be independently sampled and released.
2. Qualify two upstream sources in parallel.
3. Move volume only after two clean lots and repeat demand.
4. Never finance source uncertainty with customer preorders.

### 2. Testing and public proof

Forum customers repeatedly say a generic COA is insufficient. They look for:

- A current test
- A public lab verification path
- The same lot on the vial and report
- The vendor named as the test client when possible
- Identity, content, and purity
- Evidence covering more than the one "hero" vial
- A clear response when a result fails

Current public examples:

- [Peptide Partners](https://peptide.partners/independent-certifications/) publishes current lot-linked test files across multiple test types.
- [EDGE PEP](https://edgepep.com/pages/third-party-test-results) publishes a multi-lab report library and offers credit for customer testing.
- Current forum discussions most often mention Janoshik, TrustPointe, MZ Biolabs, and Freedom Diagnostics, but community reputation is not a substitute for lab diligence.

The trust object Noli should build is a lot passport:

- Product
- Lot
- Test date
- Lab
- Verification link
- Identity result
- Net content
- Purity
- Applicable safety panel
- Release status
- Release owner
- Remediation note if anything failed

### 3. Payments

The processor stack has four distinct layers:

1. Checkout
2. Gateway or token vault
3. Merchant account and acquiring bank
4. Settlement bank

Founders often name a gateway such as Authorize.net or NMI as if it is approval. It is not. The acquiring bank's written underwriting decision is what matters.

Current [Stripe guidance](https://support.stripe.com/questions/prohibited-and-restricted-businesses-list-faqs) says some research-purpose peptides may be supported with preventive measures, while prescription-only products can require preapproval. Current forum posts still report onboarding rejection, later termination, reserves, and frozen settlement. The correct conclusion is to obtain explicit written approval for the exact model, not to assume automatic approval or automatic prohibition.

One current Reddit sales community repeatedly presents MCC 5169 as a universal "legit route" for RUO brands. Mastercard's current [merchant reference](https://www.dm.mastercard.us/content/dam/public/mastercardcom/na/global-site/documents/quick-reference-booklet-merchant.pdf) describes MCC 5169 as wholesale distribution of chemicals typically used for industrial purposes. That definition does not itself approve a DTC peptide catalog. Merchant classification belongs to the acquiring bank after review of the exact business. A code copied from a forum is not underwriting.

Before launch, document:

- Exact approved SKU list
- Correct merchant category
- Approved product claims and site
- Recurring-billing approval
- Reserve percentage and release schedule
- Settlement timing
- Monthly volume cap
- Average and maximum ticket
- Chargeback and refund thresholds
- Descriptor
- Personal guarantee
- Termination and hold terms
- Gateway portability and token ownership
- Prohibited geographies
- Advance notice required before volume changes

Track every dollar:

`authorization -> capture -> processor balance -> reserve -> settlement -> operating bank`

Cash planning:

- Hold at least one full reorder plus payroll and refunds outside unsettled revenue.
- Stress test a 10% rolling reserve and 30 to 90 day hold.
- Notify the processor before large volume jumps.
- Use one fully approved primary and one fully approved backup.
- Do not count crypto, manual payment apps, or ACH as frictionless card equivalents.

The separate [gray-market operator appendix](./gray-market-operator-evidence.md) preserves public evidence about miscoding, cloaking, account rotation, and other workarounds without converting them into instructions.

### 4. Store and data

WooCommerce appeared frequently in the reviewed store sample, likely because it gives operators more control over gateways, hosting, extensions, and data. The store-level coding table and selection method are not yet published, so this report does not claim a representative market share.

The minimum resilient stack:

- Self-controlled domain and DNS
- Portable product and order database
- Exportable customer consent records
- Independent backups
- Explicitly approved payment integration
- Lot-level inventory
- Basic fraud controls
- Event tracking from source to settlement
- Portable email flows
- No dependence on one app for customer history

Klaviyo's [April 2026 acceptable-use policy](https://www.klaviyo.com/legal/acceptable-use-policy) reserves broad suspension rights and downstream-provider restrictions for illegal or controlled substances. Budai reports that one peptide client was removed and rebuilt in a self-hosted system. The lesson is portability, not a blanket platform recommendation.

### 5. Fulfillment

Current peptide-focused 3PL pages advertise:

- WooCommerce integration
- Lot traceability
- White-label packing
- Temperature-controlled storage
- Same-day or two-day ship SLAs
- Kitting
- Subscription fulfillment

Examples include [Flow Fulfillment](https://flowfulfillment.com/), [3PL Guys](https://3plguys.com/articles/white-label-peptide-fulfillment), and [PepFulfill](https://www.pepfulfill.com/). Treat all service claims as vendor claims until audited.

3PL acceptance test:

- Quarantine inventory on receipt
- Scan lot at receiving and shipping
- Block unreleased inventory
- Pick accuracy
- Same-day cutoff performance
- Tracking event latency
- Temperature and excursion handling
- Damaged and lost parcel workflow
- Returns and destruction workflow
- Recall report by lot and customer
- Inventory reconciliation
- Customer-data controls
- Holiday and surge plan

Do not founder-pack past the smallest proof cohort. A 3PL is part of the product experience.

### 6. Support and recovery

Current Trustpilot and Reddit reviews repeatedly reward:

- Fast answers
- Clear tracking
- Proactive delay communication
- Fast reship
- Professional refunds or credits

They repeatedly punish:

- Ghosting
- Dismissive answers
- "Google it" support
- Missing orders
- Unexplained delays
- Refund promises without settlement

Support is acquisition because every resolved failure can become a review, referral, and retained customer.

Minimum SLA:

- First human response in one business day
- Tracking issue triage in one business day
- Reship or refund decision within two business days
- Product and lot complaint escalated immediately
- One owner for every unresolved ticket

Support staff must not improvise health or product-use advice.

## Illustrative unit economics

An 80% product gross margin is plausible when a product is inexpensive relative to retail. It is not the same as an 80% business margin.

Illustrative $150 settled order:

| Cost | Creator-led order | Paid-search order |
|---|---:|---:|
| Settled revenue | $150.00 | $150.00 |
| Landed product | ($15.00) | ($15.00) |
| Testing and packaging allocation | ($5.00) | ($5.00) |
| Fulfillment and shipping subsidy | ($10.00) | ($10.00) |
| Processing and reserve cost, 8% | ($12.00) | ($12.00) |
| Refund, dispute, reship provision, 5% | ($7.50) | ($7.50) |
| Support allocation | ($5.00) | ($5.00) |
| Creator commission, 20% | ($30.00) | $0.00 |
| Paid acquisition | $0.00 | ($60.00) |
| Illustrative CM2 | $65.50 | $35.50 |
| CM2 margin | 43.7% | 23.7% |

This is a sensitivity example, not a forecast. Actual processor pricing, reserves, product specification, shipping, discounts, and acquisition can move the result dramatically.

The key lesson:

> A $4 vial can still produce a weak business if the company pays too much for trust, transactions, recovery, or attention.

The full [viability, economics, and exit model](./viability-economics-exit.md) tests bear, base, and bull cases at 50, 500, and 5,000 monthly orders. Its base case produces about $55 CM2 per $150 order before fixed overhead. The modeled base cash buffer is about $51,000 at 50 monthly orders, $206,000 at 500, and $1.36 million at 5,000 because inventory, quality, contingency, and rolling reserves grow with volume.

A genuine $50,000 daily business can economically support a $15 million price, but the public record reviewed here does not verify a completed $14 million or $15 million RUO peptide ecommerce sale. At common main-street ecommerce multiples, a $15 million value would require roughly $3.8 million to $4.6 million of normalized annual owner earnings. Treat screens and listings as diligence leads until orders reconcile through settlements, bank cash, tax records, working capital, and buyer terms.

## Noli architecture

Noli is the operating company. OVO Talent, OVO Academy, and OVO Labs remain separate support layers with explicit contracts, data boundaries, and responsibilities.

### Noli

Owns:

- Brand and approved offer
- Merchant and counterparty agreements
- Nonclinical customer experience
- Nonclinical support
- Attribution and consented marketing data
- Refund and dispute coordination

In a genuine qualified-research B2B lane, Noli can also own the product, supplier, lot release, fulfillment, and merchant account after specialist clearance and counterparty approval.

### Clinical partners

For a human-use offer, independent licensed counterparts own:

- Patient eligibility
- Clinical intake and health data
- Prescribing and treatment decisions
- Pharmacy dispensing and quality
- Patient safety and adverse-event handling
- State coverage and clinical records

Noli does not influence those decisions or store patient health data.

### OVO Talent

Owns:

- Creator recruiting
- Opt-in partner management
- Content briefs
- Claim and disclosure review
- Performance coaching
- Payout communication

It does not become merchant of record.

### OVO Academy

After proof, Academy can train and hire operators for:

- Creator onboarding
- Attribution hygiene
- Content repurposing
- Lifecycle execution
- Payout QA
- Support process QA

Academy is not a peptide course, a customer acquisition promise, a source of free labor, or a place for product-use advice.

### OVO Labs

Softwareize only after the same manual workflow repeats:

- Creator and content attribution
- Settled CM2
- Payout reconciliation
- Cohort and reorder intelligence
- Creative learning library

Do not build a dashboard before the spreadsheet hurts.

## The fastest responsible 90-day plan

### Days 1 to 14: make checkout real

- Fix one lawful lane and one eligible offer with specialist counsel.
- Obtain written approval from the exact processor and every regulated counterparty.
- For clinical care, name and diligence the provider and pharmacy before recruitment.
- For qualified-research B2B, release one independently tested lot and verify the buyer controls.
- Build one calm education, eligibility, and proof page.
- Configure seven portable lifecycle flows.
- Recruit five supervised creators only after the lane and scripts are approved.
- Create the daily scorecard.

### Days 15 to 30: run the truth cohort

- Cap at 50 to 75 fulfilled orders.
- Use creators and warm demand only inside the approved lane.
- Charge clinical customers only after provider approval.
- Resolve every support issue manually.
- Reconcile authorization to bank.
- Publish the applicable lot, pharmacy, or quality proof.
- Keep patient health data out of Noli systems.
- Pay creators accurately.

### Days 31 to 60: earn repeatability

- Run the same cohort again.
- Improve the offer and eligibility page from objections and tickets.
- Test a simple quantity ladder.
- Measure reorder intent and actual repeat.
- Confirm long-term provider, pharmacy, supplier, and capacity as applicable.
- Add support coverage.

### Days 61 to 90: add one scale lever

Choose one:

- High-intent Google
- Ten more creators
- One adjacent SKU

Do not add all three at once.

## Kill and pause conditions

Pause scale when:

- The processor is not explicitly underwriting the exact business.
- Settlements do not reconcile.
- A lot fails or cannot be matched to public proof.
- The 3PL cannot block unreleased inventory.
- Support backlog exceeds the SLA.
- Refunds, reships, or disputes erase contribution.
- Creators need claims outside the approved brief to convert.
- Paid acquisition is positive in-platform but negative after settlement and all variable costs.
- OVO Talent or OVO Academy core delivery deteriorates.

## Bottom line

The fast-money version is viable but brittle. The valuable version is a trust, distribution, customer, and operating system that can survive a supplier, processor, platform, or molecule change.

Noli should not try to win by finding the cheapest vial. It should win by being the company that can repeatedly turn trusted creators into settled contribution while making every lot, order, payout, and customer recovery auditable.
