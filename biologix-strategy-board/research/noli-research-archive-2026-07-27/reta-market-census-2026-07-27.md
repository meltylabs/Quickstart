## Method

1. Seeded discovery with Peptide Compare, Finnrick, Peptide Grades, and VialVerdict.
2. Ran 12 search variants around `buy retatrutide`, `retatrutide add to cart`, `LY3437943`, `GLP-3`, US fulfillment, same-day shipping, secure checkout, and common vial sizes.
3. Normalized links to registrable domains and removed URL-level duplicates.
4. Checked live product pages, home pages, `robots.txt`, and advertised sitemaps.
5. Classified a domain as confirmed only when a current direct Retatrutide product or catalog page was visible. A product page is not the same thing as a working payment rail.
6. Classified checkout depth separately:
   - cart verified: anonymous product cart or checkout information path rendered
   - cart visible: current in-stock or add-to-cart control visible, but no cart session tested
   - gated: account, JavaScript, bot protection, or directory evidence prevented direct confirmation
7. Excluded clinics, pharmacies, content-only pages, non-US-only stores, dead domains, duplicate aliases, and sites with no current direct Retatrutide retail proof.

## Strategic implications for Noli

1. The storefront market is far denser than the 37-vendor comparison page suggests. Search and directories lag new entrants.
2. Entry is technically easy. Ten of the twelve sampled stores use WooCommerce or Shopify. Storefront code is not the moat.
3. Turnover is high. Six of twelve sampled sellers have a domain or current store launch since July 2025.
4. Product-page uptime is not checkout proof, and checkout uptime is not regulatory or processor approval. Maintain those as separate evidence fields.
5. Noli should stay narrow and operationally exceptional: batch traceability, reliable fulfillment, responsive support, retention, documented processor underwriting, and careful claims. A giant catalog is not an advantage by itself.
