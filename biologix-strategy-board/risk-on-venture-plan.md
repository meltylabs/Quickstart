## The answer in one page

Braden appears to have found an exceptional cash engine. The public evidence does **not** show that he has built an exceptional company.

The combined public, call, and transcript evidence suggests five things:

1. **Public signal + inference:** sales-informed rank activity across body composition, appearance, recovery, and longevity is consistent with a strong demand wave.
2. **Founder-reported — unverified:** acquisition is primarily trusted word of mouth through a very small affiliate group.
3. **Founder-reported — unverified:** product-level markup is extremely high.
4. **Publicly observed:** discounts and the $200 free-shipping threshold are designed to encourage multi-item, $200-plus baskets; public data does not prove actual AOV.
5. **Founder-reported + strategic inference:** Braden appears to carry product, selling, fulfillment, and management personally, creating operating-maturity gaps Alex could address.

The opportunity is not “find his Chinese source and copy his WordPress site.” Those are the least defensible pieces. The opportunity is to turn accidental demand into a measured distribution system that can sell through multiple merchants and migrate into regulated products when the current channel tightens.

If I deliberately accepted Braden-level risk, I would build the **counterfactual cash engine** this way:

1. Start with **10–12 revenue-driving catalog labels**, not 52 products.
2. Organize the store around four customer desires: body composition, appearance, recovery, and energy/longevity.
3. Use one low-friction entry item, two hero categories, three basket builders, and three premium bundles to target a $225–$275 average order.
4. Recruit 25 tightly managed creators before trying to recruit 1,000 uncontrolled affiliates.
5. Pay only after settlement and subject every commission tier to a **contribution floor**, with holdbacks for refunds and disputes.
6. Track every creator, click, order, refund, lot, support issue, and payout in one source of truth.
7. Move Braden out of packing and shipping immediately; give him product, audience, and founder-community responsibilities.
8. Build a real controller function that separates sales, gross profit, operating contribution, cash received, owner distributions, and inventory value.
9. Treat quality and claims governance as core operating systems, not website disclaimers.
10. Capture the audience, affiliate contracts, attribution software, content library, and lawful successor products in a durable NewCo.

What I would **actually execute first** is a barbell:

- **Track A — fastest learning and cash:** a prepaid source-of-truth sprint inside Biologix, followed by President/COO economics only after the records reconcile.
- **Track B — independent leverage:** an OVO-owned creator-commerce control plane, longevity audience, and low-cost tests that translate the relative appearance signal into possible lawful successor offers.

That gives Alex the upside of the current wave without making his only asset a replaceable vial catalog.

The counterfactual launch assortment should be capped at 10–12 catalog labels until actual contribution and repeat data justify expansion.

If the exercise is strictly “same current risk profile, but better,” the **modeled opening set** is the current public top 10: GLP-3R/retatrutide, BAC water, GHK-CU, tesamorelin, MOTS-c, GLP-2T/tirzepatide, NAD+, GLOW, BPC-157 + TB-500, and KLOW. Ranks 11–15 remain a measured test queue rather than day-one complexity. ### 2. Price and basket architecture

Use a simple three-level price ladder:

| Layer | Modeled price band | Job |
|---|---:|---|
| Entry | $25–$49 | Convert new traffic and create a second-item opportunity |
| Core | $69–$129 | Carry most unit contribution |
| Premium | $149–$349 | Lift AOV without requiring more customers |

Target economics:

- AOV: $225–$275.
- Product-only gross margin: 80–85% if source-verified.
- Fully loaded operating contribution: 40–55%, not 80–85%.
- Discount and commission may never be analyzed separately; use a combined margin floor.
- Free-shipping threshold should sit just below the target basket and be tested against contribution, not copied blindly.
- Never stack a top affiliate rate, maximum quantity discount, free shipping, and a promotional code without a contribution check.

Do not write human-use “protocols” or dosing bundles. Merchandise by neutral customer-interest category and price tier, with claims and intended-use language approved before publication.

### 3. Funnel architecture

The current word-of-mouth engine should become a measurable loop:

`creator story → approved education → category page → consented lead capture → offer → paid order → fulfillment → support → repeat signal → review/referral → creator payout`

Every transition needs an event and an owner. The minimum events are:

- `creator_click`
- `landing_view`
- `lead_consent_recorded`
- `product_view`
- `checkout_started`
- `order_paid`
- `order_settled`
- `order_refunded`
- `chargeback_opened`
- `lot_shipped`
- `support_case_opened`
- `repeat_order_paid`
- `referral_order_paid`
- `commission_accrued`
- `payout_approved`
- `claim_asset_approved`
- `live_post_archived`
- `takedown_requested`

The funnel should report revenue and contribution by creator, content asset, SKU, first-order cohort, repeat cohort, and lot.

### 4. Affiliate engine

Braden’s small affiliate group may be the entire moat today. Scaling it without controls would also scale claims, refund, payment, and platform risk.

#### Program design

| Tier | Entry rule | Illustrative payout | Purpose |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Scout | Approved application, contract, training | 20% of commission base | Test fit and content discipline |
| Builder | Contribution and claims thresholds met | 25% | Reward repeatable performance |
| Partner | Strong cohort quality and clean compliance history | 30% | Retain proven creators |
| Strategic | Negotiated anchor relationship | Up to 35% on defined incremental volume | Win scarce distribution without permanent margin leakage |

Payout rates are modeled ranges, not a recommendation for any specific product or healthcare referral arrangement. Regulated care requires a separate state-by-state fee/referral analysis.

#### Payout formula

Use:

`gross product revenue at checkout - discounts - refunded product revenue - chargebacks - taxes - excluded/pass-through shipping = commission base`

Then apply the creator’s current rule version. Accrue provisionally at purchase, approve only after processor settlement and the return/dispute hold, and issue a creator-level statement showing orders, deductions, reversals, rate, and payout. Never subtract an item twice merely because a dashboard already reports “net” revenue.

#### Non-negotiable controls

- Identity, tax form, signed agreement, disclosure training, and claims training before activation.
- Every planned asset submitted against a versioned approved-claims brief.
- Capture the live post and comments after publication.
- Same-day takedown path for prohibited claims.
- No private dosing support, injection guidance, disease claims, fabricated testimonials, or hidden incentives.
- No perpetual downline commission. A one-time recruiting bounty can be considered; do not build an MLM.
- Do not pay for a regulated prescription or clinical referral until counsel approves the exact state and flow.

### 7. Operations and fulfillment

The founder must leave the packing station before growth accelerates.

#### Immediate design

- Dedicated operations lead owns demand planning, purchasing records, receiving, quarantine, release, pick/pack SLA, and inventory reconciliation.
- Qualified 3PL or properly controlled internal operation handles fulfillment.
- No sellable inventory enters available stock before lot release.
- Inventory ledger separates ordered, inbound, received, quarantined, released, allocated, shipped, returned, destroyed, and recalled units.
- Daily reconciliation: store orders → processor settlements → inventory movement → shipping status.
- Weekly cycle counts for A items; monthly full reconciliation.
- Four-hour lot-to-customer and customer-to-lot traceability target.
- Written complaint, adverse-event, quarantine, recall, and business-continuity procedures.

#### Capacity math from the screenshot scenario

At 216 orders per day, even a strong small operation requires approximately:

- 27 orders per labor-hour across an eight-hour day if one person did nothing else; or
- roughly 7 orders per labor-hour across four full-time packers before receiving, support, exceptions, and management.

That makes “Braden plus an intern” a constraint, not a virtue. The better model gives the founder zero routine fulfillment shifts.

## Sourcing and quality architecture

The correct shortcut is to source **qualified counterparties**, not to hunt for a cheap API list.

For any product, the order of operations is:

1. Classify the exact product, route, claims, customer, states, and seller role.
2. Name every legal actor: manufacturer, importer, seller, prescriber, dispenser, Responsible Person, data controller, and adverse-event owner.
3. Build the vendor longlist from official registries and state boards.
4. Screen entities, ownership, licenses, inspections, Form 483s, warning letters, recalls, discipline, exclusions, litigation, and insurance.
5. Audit the actual facility and its subcontractors.
6. Qualify representative commercial lots with independent verification appropriate to the product.
